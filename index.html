Lizoňová Lab

Drug Delivery and Controlled Release

We design carriers that deliver drugs where they are needed, at the rate they are needed. Our work spans injectable and implantable drug depot systems for controlled release, magnetically triggered and stimuli-responsive platforms, and inhalable drug carriers, designed for either systemic or local drug action. Every formulation is evaluated in our own advanced in vitro tissue models.

This research group is part of the Department of Chemical Engineering, Faculty of Chemical Engineering, University of Chemistry and Technology, Prague (UCT Prague), and works closely with the pharmaceutical industry and with institutes of the Czech Academy of Sciences.

Ing. Denisa Lizoňová, Ph.D.

Group leader · Assistant Professor, Department of Chemical Engineering, UCT Prague

Denisa Lizoňová works in drug delivery with a central focus on controlled release — designing systems that release their cargo at the right place, time and rate. She develops advanced drug formulations utilizing nano- and microparticles, magnetic nanoparticles, hydrogel systems, polymers and biomolecules, as well as drug nanocrystals for bioavailability enhancement. Her systems target various routes of administration, ranging from inhalational and parenteral to locally acting formulations.

All formulations are evaluated in advanced in vitro models: the group runs its own tissue culture laboratory with 3D and air–liquid interface cell models.

She is the principal investigator of a national research grant funded by the Czech Health Research Council (AZV ČR), has independently led research projects, supervised bachelor's, master's and doctoral students, and authored peer-reviewed publications in international journals.
